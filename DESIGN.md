## Brand & Style

The design system projects elite athletic performance, surgical precision, and bespoke luxury. It discards noisy, aggressive gym tropes in favor of an editorial, high-performance aesthetic inspired by luxury horology, contemporary athletic apparel, and architectural monograph publishing. 

The visual style blends **High-Contrast Editorial** with **Tactile Light Surfaces**:
- **Razor-sharp structural discipline:** Subtle hairline rules (1px dark/10) segment layouts like architectural drafts.
- **Monochromatic intentionality:** Neutral and grayscale accents are wielded surgically for critical actions, states, and telemetry data rather than flood fills.
- **Atmospheric depth:** Clean platinum and silver planes layered over faint monochromatic filmic grain create immersive physical weight.
- **Micro-tactical detailing:** Technical data readouts, monospaced metadata badges, index numbers (e.g., `01 // ASSESSMENT`), and precision crosshair markers elevate routine interactions into an elite training experience.

## Colors

The palette establishes an uncompromising light environment engineered for visual focus and extreme contrast.

- **Primary (`#777777` — Neutral Gray):** The kinetic ignition point. Reserved exclusively for primary conversion CTAs, active status indicators, key metric highlights, and focused interactive states. Never dilute with excessive usage across vast surface areas.
- **Secondary (`#787776` — Slate Gray):** The primary structural surface tone. Powers cards, navigation bars, modal containers, and elevated planes, distinct from the void background.
- **Tertiary (`#4d4949` — Deep Charcoal):** The editorial voice. Deployed for secondary descriptors, sub-captions, technical parameters, and inactive border treatments. Guarantees legible contrast without distracting from primary headings.
- **Neutral (`#797776` — Muted Neutral):** The infinite canvas ground. Used for page root backgrounds and inset recessed wells.
- **Text Primary (`#1a1b1c` — Deep Ink):** The primary typographic ink. High-contrast, crisp reading layer across all major titles and data readouts.
- **Hairline Accent (`rgba(26, 27, 28, 0.08)` to `rgba(26, 27, 28, 0.12)`):** Defines precise borders and grid divider lines.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs three distinct typefaces to convey authority, editorial craft, and athletic engineering:

1. **Display & Headlines (`Anybody`):** Sculptural, bold, and unapologetically confident. Tightly tracked (`-0.03em` to `-0.04em`) to create monolithic, poster-like header lockups. Large numerals use tabular sizing when paired with kinetic stats.
2. **Body & Interface (`Be Vietnam Pro`):** Clean, balanced, and ergonomic. Delivers effortless legibility for coaching methodologies, trainer biographies, and consultation forms.
3. **Data, Indices & Micro-Badges (`Space Grotesk`):** Technical and utilitarian. Formatted in uppercase with generous tracking (`0.08em` to `0.14em`) for phase labels (e.g., `PHASE_01 // STRENGTH`), category pills, and schedule metadata.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an architectural 12-column grid intersected by hairline dividers, moving between dense informational telemetry and expansive negative space.

- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - Desktop (1280px+): 12 columns, 40px gutters (`2.5rem`), dynamic outer margins with a 1440px maximum content container.
  - Tablet (768px - 1279px): 8 columns, 24px gutters (`1.5rem`), 40px margins.
  - Mobile (320px - 767px): 4 columns, 16px gutters (`1rem`), 20px margins.
- **Editorial Structure Lines:** Sections are punctuated by horizontal and vertical structural lines (`border-t`, `border-r`, `border-b` using `rgba(26, 27, 28, 0.08)`), evoking editorial print grids.
- **Rhythmic Cadence:** Components enforce vertical pacing using strict increments of `0.5rem` (8px), scaling from tight component clusters (`space-xs` / `space-sm`) to cinematic section margins (`space-xl` / 48px to 96px).

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established via tactile tonal layering, semi-translucent composite materials, and disciplined photonic glows rather than muddy dropshadows.

1. **Surface Tiers:**
   - **Ground Tier (Canvas):** `#797776` with 3% ambient monochromatic noise.
   - **Container Tier (Panels / Cards):** `#787776` backdrop at 85% opacity with `20px` backdrop-filter blur and a 1px perimeter stroke (`rgba(26, 27, 28, 0.08)`).
   - **Floating Tier (Navigation & Overlays):** `#4d4949` at 92% opacity, `32px` backdrop blur, anchored by a hairline lower border.
2. **Restrained Photonic Glows:** Interactive states or hero accents project subtle radial glows: `box-shadow: 0 0 40px -10px rgba(74, 74, 74, 0.18)`.
3. **Ghost Borders:** Cards, inputs, and modules rely on subtle 1px directional hairline borders (`rgba(26, 27, 28, 0.10)`) rather than blurred drop shadows, preserving an ultra-crisp technical silhouette.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **softer precision** logic (Level 1):
- Base interactive elements (cards, containers, inputs) use sharp 4px (`0.25rem`) corner radiuses for an engineered, industrial finish.
- Secondary modules and elevated light cards scale up to a maximum of 8px (`0.5rem`).
- Primary conversion actions utilize sharp pill capsules (full radius), contrasting against the strict rectangular matrix of the editorial grid.
- Badges and micro-tags leverage precise 2px to 4px chamfered or rounded edges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Kinetic CTA:** Full pill capsule (`rounded-full`), solid `#777777` fill, `#ffffff` bold Space Grotesk uppercase text. Hover: 2px upward translation with an ambient neutral aura (`0 0 32px rgba(119, 119, 119, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary Light Action:** 4px radius (`rounded-sm`), `#787776` background with a 1px border (`rgba(26, 27, 28, 0.15)`), `#1a1b1c` text. Hover: border transitions to `#777777`, text darkens to pure black.
- **Editorial Ghost Button:** Clean rectangular outline with zero shadow, pure typography, and an animated right arrow icon that translates +4px on interaction.

### Badges & Micro-Telemetry Chips
- **Tactical Meta Chips:** `#787776` fill, 1px border (`rgba(26, 27, 28, 0.1)`), 4px radius. Contains `Space Grotesk` uppercase text (`11px`) preceded by a pulsing 6px `#777777` status orb.
- **Pill Filter Chips:** Full-pill silhouette, transparent default with muted border. Active state: `#777777` text color with high-contrast inner border.

### Editorial Container Cards
- Constructed with Light Silver (`#787776`) at 80% opacity overlaid with `24px` backdrop blur.
- Outer border: 1px continuous hairline stroke (`rgba(26, 27, 28, 0.08)`).
- Interior: Features technical index anchors (e.g., `01 / HYPERTROPHY`) in top-right or top-left corners styled in `Space Grotesk`.

### Consultation Form Elements
- **Input Fields:** Recessed `#797776` fill, 1px perimeter border (`rgba(26, 27, 28, 0.12)`), 4px border radius. Padding: `16px 20px`.
- **Focus State:** 1px stroke transitions sharply to `#777777` with an inner 1px glow line.
- **Field Labels:** `label-caps` typography stacked 8px above input, accompanied by optional numeric indicators (e.g., `01. YOUR GOAL`).

### Stats Counters
- Monolithic `display-xl` numerals rendered in `#1a1b1c` with trailing neutral symbols (`+`, `%`, `KG`).
- Anchored directly over fine horizontal rule dividers with micro descriptive labels beneath in `label-mono`.

### Checkbox & Radio Selectors
- Custom 18px square (checkbox) or circle (radio) with 1px border (`rgba(26, 27, 28, 0.2)`).
- Selected: Solid `#777777` interior with a recessed `#ffffff` checkmark or center pin.
